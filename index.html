Executive summary

What this campaign is

A global enablement and demand campaign built around the 2026 Client Value Assessment Report — an independent benchmarking study of more than 80 IFS Copperleaf clients across five continents, collectively managing over $100 billion in capital annually.

Why it exists

Asset-intensive organizations are entering a decade of unprecedented capital investment, and buying committees increasingly demand proof, not promises. This report gives every customer-facing team a credible, quantified answer to "does this actually work?"

Why it's different from a typical whitepaper

It isn't a point-of-view piece or a set of hand-picked anecdotes. It's aggregated, benchmarked outcome data across a large, diverse client base — which is exactly what skeptical CFOs, regulators, and boards respond to.

Why benchmark reports build trust

Prospects discount vendor claims. They don't discount a pattern of results repeated across 80+ organizations, multiple industries, and five continents. Benchmarking converts a sales pitch into an evidence base.

Executive narrative

Why this report matters

The industry backdrop that makes value-based, optimized capital planning an urgent, board-level priority — not a nice-to-have.

The capital investment wave

Utilities alone are projected to invest more than $1.4 trillion through 2030 to modernize infrastructure, expand grid capacity, support electrification, and meet regulatory and ESG obligations — while still balancing reliability, resilience, affordability, and shareholder expectations.

Legacy tools have run out of road

Siloed spreadsheets and static, ranking-based prioritization cannot manage enterprise-wide portfolios worth billions of dollars. The complexity of modern capital decisions has outgrown the tools most organizations still rely on.

Seven forces converging on capital planning

Regulatory complexity

Boards and regulators expect quantified, defensible rationale for every dollar requested — not narrative justification.

Planning maturity

Organizations that formalize asset hierarchy, ownership, and governance make more consistent decisions cycle over cycle.

Decision transparency

Stakeholders want to see, and challenge, the trade-offs behind a plan — not just the plan itself.

Optimization vs. prioritization

Independent research (University of Southampton) found mathematical optimization outperforms ranking-based prioritization by 7–20%.

Scenario planning

Leaders increasingly need to test funding levels, timing, and constraints before committing capital — not after.

Value-based investment planning

A shared, quantitative definition of value turns portfolio debates from subjective to defensible.

Sales & sales engineering

Sales enablement

Pitches, discovery questions, ROI talking points, and objection handling — ready to use in the next call.

Elevator pitch (10 seconds)

IFS Copperleaf helps asset-intensive organizations turn billion-dollar capital portfolios into optimized, defensible investment plans — and the 2026 benchmark study shows clients realize $48M in average annual enterprise value doing it.

30-second pitch

Capital-intensive organizations are under pressure to invest more, justify every dollar, and hit reliability, regulatory, and ESG targets at the same time. Most are still using spreadsheets and static ranking to make billion-dollar decisions. IFS Copperleaf replaces that with value-based optimization — and independent benchmarking across 80+ clients shows 9% higher portfolio value, 30% faster planning, and 20%+ better funding outcomes.

2-minute pitch / executive conversation guide

Open with the pressure: Reference the $1.4T utility capital wave through 2030, and the fact that legacy prioritization tools weren't built for portfolios this large or this scrutinized.

Introduce the shift: Move the conversation from "prioritization" to "optimization" — the University of Southampton found optimization delivers 7–20% more portfolio value than ranking-based approaches under identical constraints.

Prove it: Cite the benchmark: 80+ clients, 5 continents, $100B+ capital managed, $48M average annual enterprise value, 96 bps ROIC lift in 3 years.

Close with a next step: Offer a tailored Business Value Assessment to quantify what these outcomes would look like inside their own portfolio.

Discovery questions

  • How is your organization defining "value" across capital projects today — consistently, or differently by region or business unit?
  • How much of your current capital plan is built through ranking or cut-line prioritization versus true optimization?
  • When funding gets cut or reallocated, how long does it take to understand the risk and service impact?
  • How defensible is your current plan in front of a regulator, board, or investor — can you quantify trade-offs on demand?
  • How connected is your planning process to actual execution and delivery feedback?
  • What would a 9% improvement in portfolio value or a 30% planning productivity gain be worth to your organization this year?

Renewals

Reframe BVAs around the study's five measurement dimensions: capital efficiency, OPEX/maintenance, planning productivity, execution accuracy, and funding/regulatory outcomes.

Roadmap conversations

Operating model

Campaign governance

Ownership, consistency rules, and the KPIs this campaign is measured against.

Campaign ownership

The Global Campaigns Team owns the master narrative, core assets, and this hub. Regional Marketing owns localization within the approved messaging framework. Product Marketing owns messaging accuracy against the source report.

How regional teams should use it

Localize the regional playbook's message and CTA; do not alter core statistics, the message hierarchy, or the Copperleaf Value Framework positioning without Product Marketing sign-off.

Brand & messaging consistency

All content must follow IFS brand guidelines (Midnight Purple / Euclid Circular A / pill CTAs) and the approved Copperleaf positioning: lead with optimization and the Value Framework, never reduce Copperleaf to a scoring or workflow tool.

Asset governance & version control

The 2026 Client Value Assessment Report PDF in SharePoint is the single source of truth for all statistics. Any derivative asset (deck, blog, ad) must cite figures exactly as published in the report — no rounding or extrapolation beyond what's stated.
